How to open an XML invoice on iPhone?

In Mail or Files, touch the file, then the Share button, then "Open in Lisible". Or open Lisible and touch "Open a file".

Sample files

XRechnung (CII) · Peppol (UBL) · FatturaPA signed (.p7m) · KSeF FA(3)

Is the signature checked?

Lisible shows who signed a .p7m file and when, and the document inside. It does not judge the legal validity of the signature: official verifiers do that.
