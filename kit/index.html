Readable e-invoices inside your own software.

Lisible Kit turns XRechnung, ZUGFeRD, Factur-X, Peppol UBL, FatturaPA and KSeF files, signed .p7m envelopes and Outlook winmail.dat or .msg e-mails into a clean document your users can read. It runs in the browser or in your iOS and macOS app. Nothing leaves the device.

Try it

What you get

JavaScript widget

One 45 KB file, no dependency. Drop in <lisible-viewer>, or call read() and get structured data back.

Swift package

The engine of the Lisible app for iOS 16+ and macOS 13+. DocumentLoader.load(data, name:) returns the invoice, the e-mail or the signature.

Formats of six countries

Germany, France, Italy, Belgium, Poland, Peppol everywhere. Credit notes, discounts and charges, withholding tax, prepaid amounts.

Private by design

No server, no upload, no tracking. Invoices carry bank details: they stay with your user.

Two lines

<script src="lisible-kit.js"></script>
<lisible-viewer lang="de"></lisible-viewer>
const kit = LisibleKit.create({ lang: "fr" });
const doc = kit.read(bytes, "fattura.xml.p7m");
doc.invoice.gross;            // 366
doc.signature.signers;        // names from the certificates
element.innerHTML = kit.html(doc);
