What is a P7M file?

A digitally signed document (CAdES / PKCS#7 envelope). Italian e-invoices often arrive as .xml.p7m; Lisible extracts and lays out the document inside.

How do I open a P7M or XML invoice on iPhone or Mac?

With the Lisible app: share the file from Mail or Files → “Open in Lisible”. It works offline and exports invoices as PDF.

Is the signature validated?

Lisible shows who signed and when; the legal validity of the signature is for official verifiers.

Is it free?

Yes: reading is free, here and in the app. No account, no ads.
