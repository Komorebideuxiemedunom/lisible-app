Privacy policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026. Version française

Your files never leave your device. No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no cookies.

The Lisible app (iPhone, iPad, Mac)

Invoices and signed files you open are read on your device only. Lisible keeps a copy of recently opened files on the device so you can reopen them; you can remove them from the list at any time. Nothing is uploaded.

Lisible Pro in the app is purchased through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details.

The website and Lisible Pro for the web

Files. The online viewers read your files inside your browser. They are never uploaded, stored or sent anywhere. Each page carries a Content-Security-Policy that makes the browser refuse any connection except to the licence service named below, so a file cannot be sent elsewhere even by mistake. Closing the tab erases everything that was read.

What stays in your browser. If you activate Lisible Pro, your browser stores the licence key and a random activation number (local storage). Nothing else is stored. “Remove the key” deletes both and frees the activation.

What is sent, and to whom. When you activate a key, and then about once a week, the page sends the licence key and the random activation number to Lemon Squeezy's licence service to check that the key is valid. No file, no file content, no name and no device information is sent.

Purchases. Lisible Pro for the web and Lisible Kit are sold by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as merchant of record: it processes the payment, VAT and invoice under its own privacy policy. We never see your card details. We receive the order details Lemon Squeezy shares with sellers (name, e-mail address, country, product, amount, licence key). We use them only to deliver the licence, provide support and keep our accounts.

How long. Order records are kept for 10 years, the period French accounting law requires (Code de commerce, article L123-22). Support e-mails are deleted within 12 months after the conversation ends. Licence keys stay valid as long as the licence; on request we revoke and delete a key unless the law requires us to keep the order record.

Hosting. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, under the GitHub privacy statement. We have no access to these logs and add no analytics.

Your rights. Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by writing to the address below. You can also complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr). Legal bases: performing the sale (licence, support) and our legal accounting obligations.

Controller. Lucas Dansac, inceept@gmail.com.
