Invoices and signed files you open are read on your device only. Lisible keeps a copy of recently opened files on the device so you can reopen them; you can remove them from the list at any time. Nothing is uploaded.

Lisible Pro is purchased through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details.
