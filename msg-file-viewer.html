Open an Outlook .msg file, free

A .msg file is an e-mail saved from Outlook. Without Outlook (Mac, iPhone, Gmail) it won't open. Drop it here: Lisible shows the sender, recipients, the message and its attachments to download.

🔒 The file is read in your browser only: it is never uploaded.

Outlook e-mails on your Mac or iPhone? Lisible opens .msg, winmail.dat and .eml in one tap.

Get Lisible

How do I open a .msg file without Outlook?

Drop it on this page, or on iPhone and Mac use the Lisible app: share the file from Mail or Files → “Open in Lisible”. It works offline.

Can I get the attachments back?

Yes: each attachment can be downloaded, and an attached invoice (.xml, .p7m) opens right here.

And winmail.dat or .eml files?

Lisible opens them too: see the winmail.dat opener.

Is it free?

Yes. No account, no ads, and your files stay on your device.
